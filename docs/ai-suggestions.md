# AI Gift Suggestions

This guide explains how to use AI-powered gift suggestions to find the perfect present for your recipient.

## Overview

Our AI analyzes your recipient's profile and wishlist to generate personalized gift ideas that fit within the group's budget.

## How It Works

The AI considers:

1. **Profile information** - Bio and interests
2. **Wishlist items** - What they've already asked for
3. **Budget range** - The group's min/max limits
4. **General preferences** - Patterns in their choices

Then generates 5 unique gift suggestions tailored to them.

## Accessing AI Suggestions

### From Your Assignment

1. View your Secret Santa assignment
2. Click **Get AI Suggestions** or **View Suggestions**
3. Or navigate to `/groups/[groupId]/suggestions`

### Requirements

- The draw must be complete
- You must have a recipient assigned
- Your recipient should have some profile info or wishlist items

## Understanding Suggestions

Each suggestion includes:

### Gift Name
A clear, specific gift idea

### Description
Details about the gift and why it would be good

### Estimated Price
Approximate cost (in the group's currency)

### Reasoning
Why the AI thinks this matches your recipient

## Tips for Better Suggestions

### Help Your Santa Get Better Suggestions for You

1. **Complete your profile** - Add a bio and interests
2. **Add wishlist items** - Even just ideas or categories
3. **Be specific** - "Fantasy novels" is better than "books"
4. **Include variety** - Different types and price points

### Using Suggestions as a Santa

1. **Use as inspiration** - Don't feel limited to exact items
2. **Combine ideas** - Mix suggestion elements
3. **Check the budget** - Verify prices fit the range
4. **Add personal touch** - Customize based on what you know

## When Suggestions Work Best

AI suggestions are most helpful when your recipient has:

- A filled-out profile with interests
- Some wishlist items (even generic ones)
- A bio describing their hobbies
- Clear preferences

## When to Look Elsewhere

If suggestions don't quite fit, try:

- Their wishlist directly
- Asking via anonymous messages
- The interests in their profile
- Generic crowd-pleasing gifts

## Suggestion Accuracy

Keep in mind:

- Suggestions are AI-generated starting points
- Prices are estimates and may vary
- Some suggestions may not be available
- Use your judgment about what fits

## Refreshing Suggestions

Want different ideas? You can:

1. Return to the suggestions page later
2. The AI may generate different options
3. Consider different aspects of their profile

## Privacy

- AI only sees public profile information
- Wishlist items visible to you
- No personal data is shared externally
- Suggestions are generated privately for you

## Combining with Other Features

### With Wishlists
- Use AI ideas to supplement their wishlist
- Find gifts similar to wishlist items
- Discover things they didn't think to ask for

### With Messages
- Ask your recipient about AI suggestions
- "Would you like something related to [interest]?"
- Get feedback without revealing specifics

### With Budget
- AI respects the group budget range
- Filter suggestions by price
- Combine smaller suggestions for bigger impact

## Troubleshooting

### "No suggestions available"
- Your recipient may need more profile info
- Try again after they update their profile
- Fall back to their wishlist

### Suggestions don't seem relevant
- Profile information may be limited
- AI does its best with available data
- Use suggestions as general inspiration

### Prices seem off
- Estimates are approximate
- Market prices vary
- Always verify before purchasing
