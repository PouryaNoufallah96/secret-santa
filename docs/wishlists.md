# Wishlists

This guide explains how to create and manage wishlists to help your Secret Santa find the perfect gift.

## Why Use Wishlists?

Wishlists make Secret Santa easier for everyone:

- **For your Santa** - Clear ideas of what you'd like
- **For you** - Higher chance of getting something you'll love
- **For everyone** - Less stress about gift selection

## Creating Your Wishlist

### Step 1: Navigate to Wishlists

You can access wishlists from:
- Your dashboard sidebar
- The group page
- Direct navigation to `/wishlists`

### Step 2: Add Items

Click **Add Item** and fill in:

**Required:**
- **Item Name** - What do you want? (e.g., "Cozy Reading Socks")

**Optional but Helpful:**
- **Description** - More details about the item
- **URL** - Link to the product online
- **Image** - Visual reference for your Santa
- **Price** - Estimated cost (helps Santa stay in budget)
- **Priority** - Low, Medium, or High

### Step 3: Set Priority

Use priority levels to guide your Santa:

- **High** (Red) - Items you really want
- **Medium** (Yellow) - Would be nice to have
- **Low** (Green) - Just ideas, no pressure

### Step 4: Assign to a Group

You can make wishlist items:
- **Group-specific** - Only visible to that group's members
- **General** - Visible across all your groups

## Managing Your Wishlist

### Editing Items

1. Find the item on your wishlist
2. Click the **Edit** button
3. Update any fields
4. Save your changes

### Deleting Items

1. Find the item on your wishlist
2. Click the **Delete** button
3. Confirm the deletion

### Organizing Items

- Use priority levels to indicate preference
- Add descriptions to clarify what you want
- Include links to make shopping easier
- Add images for visual items

## Viewing Your Recipient's Wishlist

After the draw, you can view your recipient's wishlist:

### Step 1: Go to Your Assignment

1. Navigate to your group
2. Go to the draw page
3. View your assignment

### Step 2: Access Their Wishlist

From your assignment, click **View Wishlist** to see:
- All their wishlist items for this group
- Item details and priorities
- Product links (if provided)
- Price estimates

### Step 3: Also Check Their Profile

Your recipient's profile shows:
- Their bio
- Their interests
- General preferences

This helps if their wishlist is empty or you want more ideas.

## Purchase Tracking

### Marking Items as Purchased

When you buy an item from your recipient's wishlist:

1. Go to their wishlist
2. Find the item you purchased
3. Click **Mark as Purchased**

### Why This Matters

- **Prevents duplicates** - If multiple people can see the wishlist
- **Private to you** - Your recipient won't see it's marked
- **Helpful for planning** - Track what you've bought

## Wishlist Best Practices

### For Creating Your List

1. **Add variety** - Different price points and types
2. **Be specific** - Include sizes, colors, preferences
3. **Include links** - Make shopping easy
4. **Set realistic prices** - Match the group's budget range
5. **Update regularly** - Add new items, remove outdated ones

### For Using Your Recipient's List

1. **Check the budget** - Stay within the group's limits
2. **Respect priorities** - High priority items first
3. **Read descriptions** - Details matter
4. **Don't feel limited** - The list is a guide, not a rule
5. **Use AI suggestions** - Get additional ideas

## Wishlist Privacy

- **Your items** - Only visible to your Santas
- **Recipient's items** - Only visible after the draw
- **Purchase status** - Only visible to the purchaser
- **General items** - Visible to all your groups

## Tips for Different Budgets

### Under $25
- Books, small accessories
- Consumables (candles, coffee, snacks)
- Digital subscriptions

### $25-$50
- Quality accessories
- Home goods
- Experience gifts

### $50+
- Electronics, premium items
- Multi-item gift sets
- Special experiences
