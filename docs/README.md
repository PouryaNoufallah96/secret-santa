# Secret Santa App Documentation

Welcome to Secret Santa! This app makes organizing gift exchanges easy and fun. Whether you're planning a family exchange, office party, or friend group Secret Santa, we've got you covered.

## What is Secret Santa?

Secret Santa is a gift exchange tradition where each participant is randomly assigned another participant to give a gift to. The identity of the gift-giver remains a secret until the exchange event.

## Key Features

- **Easy Group Creation** - Set up your exchange in minutes with customizable settings
- **Fair Draw Algorithm** - Guaranteed fair matching with support for exclusion rules
- **Wishlists** - Help your Secret Santa find the perfect gift
- **AI Gift Suggestions** - Get personalized gift ideas powered by AI
- **Anonymous Messaging** - Communicate with your recipient without revealing your identity
- **Event Coordination** - Manage RSVPs and share event details
- **Gift History** - Keep memories of past exchanges

## Quick Start

1. **Sign in** with your Google account
2. **Create a group** or join one with an invite code
3. **Invite members** by sharing your unique code
4. **Run the draw** when everyone has joined
5. **View your assignment** and start gift shopping!
