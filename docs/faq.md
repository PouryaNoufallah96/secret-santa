# Frequently Asked Questions

## Getting Started

### How do I sign up?
Click "Sign in with Google" on the homepage. Your account is automatically created on first sign-in.

### Is this app free?
Yes, Secret Santa is completely free to use.

### Do I need a Google account?
Currently, yes. We use Google authentication for secure, simple sign-in.

## Groups

### How many groups can I join?
There's no limit! Join as many Secret Santa exchanges as you'd like.

### Can I be in multiple groups at once?
Yes, you can participate in many groups simultaneously.

### What happens if someone leaves a group?
Before the draw, members can leave and the group continues normally. After the draw, leaving would disrupt assignments, so it's not recommended.

### Can I delete a group?
Admins can archive groups, which removes them from active view while preserving history.

## The Draw

### Is the draw really random?
Yes! Our algorithm ensures mathematically fair, random assignments.

### Can I redo the draw?
Admins can reset and redo the draw before the exchange date, but not after.

### What if the draw fails?
This usually means exclusion rules are too restrictive. Try removing some exclusions.

### Can I see who has me as their Santa?
No, that would ruin the surprise! You'll find out at the exchange.

### What if I accidentally see my assignment?
The reveal is blur-protected, but if you do see it accidentally, the surprise is still preserved for others!

## Exclusions

### What are exclusion rules?
Rules that prevent certain pairs from being matched (e.g., couples who already exchange gifts).

### Are exclusions mutual?
Yes, if A can't draw B, then B also can't draw A.

### How many exclusions can I set?
Be careful - too many exclusions can make a valid draw impossible. The system will warn you.

### Can I add exclusions after the draw?
No, exclusions must be set before running the draw.

## Wishlists

### Who can see my wishlist?
Only your Secret Santa(s) can see your wishlist items.

### Can I have different wishlists for different groups?
Yes, you can assign items to specific groups or keep them general.

### What if I don't add a wishlist?
Your Santa will rely on your profile interests and AI suggestions.

### Can I update my wishlist after the draw?
Yes! Keep updating it to help your Santa find the perfect gift.

## Messaging

### Are messages really anonymous?
Yes, your identity is completely hidden when messaging as a Santa.

### Can admins read my messages?
No, messages are private between you and your recipient/Santa.

### When can I send messages?
Only after the draw is complete.

### Will my Santa know who I am when I message them?
Yes, when you message as a recipient, they know it's you (their recipient), but you don't know who they are.

## Events

### Do I have to set event details?
No, it's optional but helpful for coordinating the exchange.

### Can everyone see RSVPs?
Yes, all group members can see who's attending.

### Can I change my RSVP?
Yes, update it anytime before the event.

## AI Suggestions

### How accurate are AI suggestions?
They're personalized starting points based on profile and wishlist data. Use them as inspiration!

### Why are suggestions not showing?
Your recipient may need more profile information or wishlist items.

### Are suggestion prices accurate?
They're estimates. Always verify actual prices before purchasing.

## Gift History

### What is gift history?
A record of past exchanges including who you gave to and received from.

### How do I add to history?
After the exchange, you can record gift details before archiving the group.

### Can I see my history across years?
Yes, the history page shows all your past exchanges.

## Privacy & Security

### Is my data secure?
Yes, we use secure authentication and encrypted connections.

### Who can see my profile?
Group members can see your name and public profile info.

### Can I delete my account?
Contact the admin for account deletion requests.

## Troubleshooting

### I can't sign in
- Make sure you're using a Google account
- Try clearing your browser cache
- Check that cookies are enabled

### I'm not receiving notifications
- Check your notification settings
- Ensure browser notifications are enabled
- Try refreshing the page

### The page won't load
- Check your internet connection
- Try a different browser
- Clear your browser cache

### I can't find my group
- Check the Groups page for all your groups
- Make sure you're signed in
- Verify the invite code was correct

### Something else isn't working
- Refresh the page
- Sign out and back in
- Contact the group admin for help

## Contact & Support

### How do I report a bug?
Contact the app administrators or report issues through the feedback system.

### How do I suggest a feature?
We welcome suggestions! Use the feedback channels to share ideas.

### Where can I get more help?
- Check this FAQ
- Contact your group admin
- Reach out to support

## Tips & Best Practices

### For Organizers
1. Set clear budget expectations
2. Give people time to join before drawing
3. Set exclusions early
4. Add event details promptly
5. Remind members to update wishlists

### For Participants
1. Complete your profile with interests
2. Add diverse wishlist items
3. Stay within budget
4. RSVP promptly
5. Have fun with anonymous messaging!

### For Great Gifts
1. Check the wishlist first
2. Use AI suggestions for inspiration
3. Consider their interests
4. Stay within budget
5. Add a personal touch
