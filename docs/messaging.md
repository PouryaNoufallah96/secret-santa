# Anonymous Messaging

This guide explains how to use the anonymous messaging system to communicate with your Secret Santa or recipient.

## How It Works

After the draw, you can send messages without revealing your identity:

- **As a Santa** - Message your recipient anonymously
- **As a Recipient** - Message your secret Santa

The identities remain hidden until the exchange!

## Accessing Messages

1. Go to your group dashboard
2. Click **Messages** in the navigation
3. Or navigate to `/groups/[groupId]/messages`

**Note:** Messaging is only available after the draw is complete.

## Message Tabs

If you're both giving and receiving in a group, you'll see two tabs:

### "As Secret Santa" Tab

Use this to message your recipient:

- They'll see messages from "Your Secret Santa"
- Your identity stays hidden
- Great for asking questions about their wishlist

### "As Recipient" Tab

Use this to message your Santa:

- They'll see messages from "Your Recipient"
- Your identity is known, but theirs isn't
- Great for giving hints or saying thanks

## Sending Messages

1. Select the appropriate tab
2. Type your message in the input box
3. Click **Send**

Messages appear in chronological order with timestamps.

## Why Use Messaging?

### As a Santa, you can:

- **Clarify wishlist items** - "What size do you wear?"
- **Get preferences** - "Any colors you don't like?"
- **Build excitement** - "I found something you'll love!"
- **Ask subtle questions** - Without revealing what you're getting

### As a Recipient, you can:

- **Add hints** - "I've been really into cooking lately"
- **Update preferences** - "I already got a new phone case"
- **Express excitement** - "Can't wait for the exchange!"
- **Say thanks** - "I appreciate you being my Santa"

## Message Etiquette

### Do:
- Be friendly and festive
- Keep the mystery alive
- Respond to questions promptly
- Have fun with it!

### Don't:
- Reveal your identity (defeats the purpose!)
- Ask probing questions to figure out who they are
- Send inappropriate content
- Spam with too many messages

## Creative Message Ideas

### For Santas:

```
"Ho ho ho! Just checking - did you want the blue or green one?"

"Your Santa has been shopping... 🎁"

"Hint: your gift might be something from your wishlist... or maybe not!"
```

### For Recipients:

```
"Dear Santa, just wanted to say I'm not picky about brands!"

"In case it helps, my favorite color is purple 💜"

"I'm SO excited for the exchange!"
```

## Message Notifications

When you receive a new message:

1. A notification appears on the bell icon
2. You can see it in your notifications list
3. Click to go directly to the messages

## Privacy

- Messages are only visible to you and the other party
- Group admins cannot read your messages
- Messages are stored securely
- Identities are protected until after the exchange

## After the Exchange

Once identities are revealed:
- You'll know who your Santa was
- The mystery is solved!
- Messages remain as a fun record

## Troubleshooting

### Can't access messages
- Make sure the draw is complete
- Verify you're a member of the group
- Check you're signed in

### Not receiving notifications
- Check your notification settings
- Ensure notifications are enabled in your browser

### Messages not sending
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure the message isn't empty
- Try refreshing the page
